not grant a general trademark or artwork licence. Startup sound is synthesized by
the project's Rust source; no downloaded song or recording is bundled.

Geist is bundled under SIL Open Font License 1.1, with its exact licence text.
The historical Manrope font asset retains its OFL text even though current painting

generated/authored by this project; they do not incorporate marketplace assets.

NOAA ETOPO1 regional/overview samples retain their source URL, retrieval date and
resampling metadata in project provenance. They are composite reference data, not
a new local survey. See the NOAA attribution stored in each regional project.

Oil evaporation (`src/spill.rs`) uses M. Fingas's published empirical
equations (Environment Canada; Fingas 2004, *Journal of Hazardous Materials*
107) and the Fay (1971) gravity–viscous spreading law; only the published
coefficients are used.

Survey accuracy limits (`src/survey.rs`) are the numeric parameters of
Table 1 of the International Hydrographic Organization's *IHO Standards for
Hydrographic Surveys*, S-44 Edition 6.2.0 (October 2024), cited, not
reproduced; the standard itself is copyright IHO.

Search-and-rescue leeway coefficients (`src/sar.rs`) are the published US
Coast Guard leeway taxonomy values: Allen, A. A. & Plourde, J. V. (1999),
*Review of leeway: field experiments and implementation*, USCG R&D Center
report CG-D-08-99, and Allen, A. A. (2005), *Leeway divergence*, CG-D-05-05,
as tabulated for SAROPS (list by A. Allen, 2010) and compiled by Ø. Breivik
(2011). Only the numeric coefficients are used; no code is copied.

Animal models are generated by this project from published body
proportions, fin layouts and colour descriptions of each family and
species; no third-party models or images are used.

Sky and water optics use published coefficients only: Bruneton & Neyret
(2008) and Hillaire (2020) for the atmosphere, NOAA's solar position
equations, Tessendorf (2001) for FFT waves, and Monahan &
O'Muircheartaigh (1980) for whitecap coverage.

Live sea states (Earth tab live waves, sea forecasts, live sea in new scenes,
seakeeping outlook, search-and-rescue drift, `--forecast`, `--sar`) are downloaded at run time from Open-Meteo
(https://open-meteo.com), CC BY 4.0: "Weather data by Open-Meteo.com", with
wave models from ECMWF, Météo-France (generated using E.U. Copernicus Marine
Service Information), NOAA NCEP and DWD. The attribution is shown wherever the
data appear. The free service is for non-commercial use; commercial users need
an Open-Meteo subscription (`PELAGIC_OPEN_METEO_KEY`). No forecast data are
bundled. Citation: Zippenfenig, P. (2023). Open-Meteo.com Weather API.
https://doi.org/10.5281/ZENODO.7970649

Every distribution includes `ThirdParty/inventory.json` and exact upstream licence
texts for the locked Rust dependencies that can be compiled into the shipped
`x86_64-pc-windows-gnu` target, including build-only entries. Dependencies used
only on other operating systems are not part of the Windows build and are not
listed. This inventory is generated from Cargo metadata; no developer-specific
filesystem paths are included. Where a published crate omits its licence file
(AccessKit, the egui family, clipboard-win, profiling, spirv, zune-core and
zune-jpeg), the exact upstream text is vendored under `third-party/` with a
`SOURCE.md` naming the commit it came from.

Core families include serde/serde_json, RustCrypto SHA-256, windows-rs/windows-sys,
AccessKit, ab_glyph/ttf-parser, png/flate2, wgpu/naga, egui/egui_dock, winit,
ureq and zune-jpeg. Their respective MIT/Apache/OFL/other
licence expressions and notices are preserved per package. Rust/LLVM-MinGW are
build tools and are not included in the application ZIP.
